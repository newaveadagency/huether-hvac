How often should I replace my furnace filter?

For filters 1″ thick or less, replace them once a month. Thicker filters can go as long as 90 days.

How often should I change the batteries in my thermostat?

Most people forget they have to. Replace them once a year, at the same time as your smoke detector batteries.

I’m remodeling a room. Do I need to do anything to the vents?

If you’re doing drywall work, mask off the vent and the cold-air return in that room. The dust will quickly clog your filter and stay in your ducts for years.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. We service manufacturing, warehousing, medical, office and multi-family buildings, from break/fix calls to new installs.
